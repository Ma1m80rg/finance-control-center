First-time connection instructions

Upload Finance_Control_Center_Data.json to your private Google Drive Finances folder, then choose it through Google's file picker. Every authorized Google account needs Editor access to that same file. On a new browser or phone, select the file once. You may also import your old JSON backup after connecting.

Install Finance Control Center

Installing creates a shortcut. It does not copy your financial data or remove Google sign-in requirements. An internet connection is needed to read and sync your private Drive file.
